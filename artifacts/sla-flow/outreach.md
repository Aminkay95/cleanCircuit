# SLA Flow organic validation package

## Ideal interview candidate

Owner-operator of a residential cleaning company with 1–9 staff who coordinates quotes, cleaners, contracts, schedules, and deposits across multiple tools.

## One-to-one invitation

Hi [first name] — I’m researching how small cleaning companies move a new customer from quote to scheduled job. I’m not selling anything on this call. Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation about your current workflow? I can share the findings afterward.

## Pilot follow-up

Thanks for explaining your workflow. We have a private prototype combining quotes, signatures, scheduling, team hand-offs, and deposits. If the problem we discussed is still relevant, would you like a guided pilot preview?

## Interview guide

1. Walk me through the last customer job from first inquiry to deposit.
2. Which steps required copying information between tools?
3. Where do delays, mistakes, or missed commitments happen most often?
4. What have you already tried, and why was it insufficient?
5. What measurable improvement would justify changing your process?

Do not pitch until the interview is complete. Record observed facts separately from interpretations. Do not claim the product saves time until pilot evidence demonstrates it.

## Pass criteria

- 10 qualified interviews completed.
- At least 6 independently describe the same hand-off problem.
- At least 3 agree to a guided pilot.
- At least 2 accept a stated paid-pilot price without a hypothetical discount.

## Stop criteria

Pause the build if fewer than 4 of 10 interviewees report the core problem, or if no interviewee accepts a paid pilot.
